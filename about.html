About W.J. Rogers

W.J. Rogers is an author from Hampton, Virginia, whose writing reflects faith, family, responsibility, and the importance of restoring value in human life.

His first book, Daddy Said…Framing the Mindset of the Next Generation, offered fatherly advice for young adults. His later work, Hellfire Effect: Thou Shalt Not Kill, invites readers into a bold fictional world designed to prompt serious conversation about human life, justice, and hope.
