HELLFIRE EFFECT WEBSITE PACKAGE
Author: W.J. Rogers

FILES INCLUDED
- index.html: Home page
- book.html: Book page
- audiobook.html: Audiobook page
- blog.html: Blog page
- media.html: News & Media page
- about.html: Author bio page
- contact.html: Contact page
- styles.css: Website styling

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in your web browser to preview the website.
3. Replace placeholder links marked with #.
4. Add your book cover by placing your image in this folder and naming it cover.jpg.
5. Upload all files to a website host.

FREE OR LOW-COST HOSTING OPTIONS
- GitHub Pages
- Netlify
- WordPress.com
- Wix
- Squarespace

SEO CHECKLIST
- Keep the phrase "Hellfire Effect: Thou Shalt Not Kill" on the home page and book page.
- Add your real purchase links.
- Add your Spotify and ElevenReader audiobook links.
- Add new blog posts regularly.
- Submit your site to Google Search Console after publishing.
- Share your website link on Facebook, LinkedIn, press releases, email signature, and author profiles.
