Author Bio

W.J. Rogers is an author from Hampton, Virginia. His work explores fatherly wisdom, faith, responsibility, and the value of human life. His book Hellfire Effect: Thou Shalt Not Kill was written to prompt meaningful conversations about violence, accountability, and hope.
