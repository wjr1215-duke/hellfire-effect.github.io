About the Book

Hellfire Effect: Thou Shalt Not Kill is a thought-provoking novel by W.J. Rogers that blends speculative fiction, faith, social commentary, and suspense.

In a society overwhelmed by violence, a mysterious force suddenly changes the rules. Those who take human life face immediate and terrifying consequences. The world responds with fear, debate, faith, science, politics, and transformation.

Genre

Christian speculative fiction, social thriller, faith-based drama, and science fiction.

Core Question

What would happen if humanity could no longer ignore the cost of taking a life?

Reader Appeal

For readers interested in faith, morality, justice, public debate, and powerful “what if” stories.
