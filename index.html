What if violence carried an immediate consequence?

Hellfire Effect: Thou Shalt Not Kill imagines a society shaken by a mysterious phenomenon that forces humanity to confront the meaning of life, justice, and accountability. Political leaders, scientists, faith communities, and ordinary people struggle to understand a world where murder becomes nearly unthinkable.

Faith

A story that invites readers to consider spiritual meaning, moral responsibility, and the commandment: Thou shalt not kill.

Science

Researchers and leaders search for answers as unexplained events transform society and challenge human understanding.

Human Value

At its heart, the book asks whether society can rediscover the sacred value of every human life.
