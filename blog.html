Welcome to the World of Hellfire Effect

Starter post

Why I Wrote Hellfire Effect

Starter post

This story was inspired by a desire to prompt meaningful conversations about restoring the value of human life and imagining how society might change if violence itself became unthinkable.

Faith, Science, and Society

Starter post

Throughout the story, scientists, political leaders, and people of faith wrestle with a phenomenon they cannot fully explain. Their reactions reveal much about fear, hope, leadership, and humanity.

Can Fiction Help Restore the Value of Human Life?

Fiction allows readers to enter a world of consequences, choices, and reflection. Hellfire Effect uses a speculative premise to encourage deeper thought about violence, accountability, and the worth of every person.
